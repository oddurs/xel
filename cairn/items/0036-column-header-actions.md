## Problem

Users expect to shape columns from the header.

## Proposal

Resize by dragging (double-click to fit), and a context menu to insert a column left or right, rename, delete, and open the column in the inspector.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Widths are saved in the workbook
- [ ] Every header action is undoable
